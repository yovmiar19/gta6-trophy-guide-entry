    About this guide

    Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list, as Exophase and PSNProfiles carry it. Every guide is written for this page in our own words, and every specific fact cites its source beside the trophy. Where no guide has confirmed a trigger yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.

    One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude, if you skip the Chapter 1 balcony meditation. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats switch trophies off. There are no online trophies. All 55 are earned in single-player.
